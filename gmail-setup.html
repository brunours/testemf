Daily News Briefing — Gmail setup

One-time connection between your Supabase news server and Gmail.

1. Create the Google OAuth client

In Google Cloud, create an OAuth 2.0 Web application and add this exact Authorized redirect URI:

https://rbwcmjtcmhnivkmoxpsj.supabase.co/functions/v1/daily-news-gmail-setup/auth/callback

2. Enter the OAuth credentials

The Client ID and Client secret are submitted directly from your browser to Supabase and stored in Supabase Vault. They are not stored on GitHub.

After you press the button, Google will ask you to select the Gmail account and approve the requested permissions.
